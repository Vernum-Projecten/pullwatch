**Branches** in the sidebar (⌘3) shows pushes to the branches of the repositories you follow, newest first. It answers a question the PRs and Tags lists cannot: who is working on what, before there is a pull request or a tag.

<Screenshot name="branches" alt="The Branches section: pushes to branches of three repositories, each with its branch name, repository, author, the number of new commits and the time of the push." />

## What a row shows

Each row has the branch, the repository, who pushed (with their picture), the commits and when. The Commits column says what happened:

- `+3`: three new commits since Pullwatch last looked.
- **Force Push**: the branch was rewritten, so its history no longer continues from before.
- **New Branch**: the branch did not exist yet.
- No label: the last commit of a branch that moved in the past 14 days. Pullwatch fills the list this way the first time it reads a repository, so the section is not empty on day one.

Pullwatch sees what changed between two reads, not each separate push. Three pushes between two reads are one row with all the new commits.

## Search and filters

Search by branch, repository or person. The toolbar filters on a repository or a person, and clicking a name in the table filters on that person too. **Others Only** hides your own pushes.

Pullwatch recognises your own pushes by your account on GitHub, and on Bitbucket and Gitea where the account is linked to the person. Otherwise it compares the name and the e-mail address of the author with yours.

Double-click a row (or choose Open in the context menu) to open the comparison at the service. Pullwatch does not show commits or files itself.

## No notifications

Pushes never notify you, do not count as unread, and do not show in the menu panel or the Dock badge. This is a list to look at, not a stream to keep up with.

## Following branches

Branches follow along with pull requests, per repository. To switch them off for a repository, clear its **Branches** checkbox in the account's list of repositories (Settings, Accounts). When no repository follows branches, the section offers **Follow Branches**.

Branches are read at most every 15 minutes, separately from pull requests and tags. A rate limit on branches never holds back pull requests or tags: those arrive as usual, and branches wait until the limit is over. Pushes are kept as long as the history (Settings, General, **Keep history**).
