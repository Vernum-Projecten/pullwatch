**Statistics** in the sidebar shows pull request activity from the history Pullwatch keeps.

<Screenshot name="stats" alt="The Statistics section: 8 pull requests opened, 2 open drafts, 1 closed without merging, a median lead time of 12 hours and 6 active authors, a bar chart per month and rankings of authors and repositories." />

- Opened and merged pull requests per week, month or year (the last 12 weeks, 12 months or 5 years).
- The pull requests opened and merged in the current period, open drafts, the median time from opening to merge, and the number of active authors.
- Rankings of authors and of the busiest repositories.

Filter on a project or an author; click a name in the ranking to select it, and from there jump to that author's pull requests or the repository's page. Bots (`[bot]` in the name) are hidden by default. **Count co-authors** in the author menu counts a pull request for each of its co-authors too.

A repository page has the same chart per month for that repository alone.

Statistics count pull requests, not commits, and go back as far as the history does (Settings, General, **Keep history**).
